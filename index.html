How it works

  1. Lock the apps. Pick the apps that eat the day, like Instagram or games. CounterWait locks them through Apple's Screen Time. For a child, you pick them on their iPhone with your passcode.
  2. Earn minutes. Push ups, squats or jumping jacks the camera counts, right on the iPhone: nothing is recorded, saved or sent. Steps, workouts, Exercise minutes and mindful minutes from Apple Health. Or a chore or task a parent/guardian approves.
  3. Spend them. Tap the minutes you want. The apps open, then lock again when the time runs out.

Your time. Your effort.

Your parent or guardian sets the rules, but you have a way to earn more screen time. Finish a task, get moving or do a workout to build up your minutes, then spend them when you choose.

Less asking for more time. More earning it yourself.

On a child's iPhone, the locks stay put. CounterWait turns off deleting apps there, so a locked app can't be deleted and installed again, and changing the clock doesn't add time. Two Apple settings close the rest: with Block New App Installs on, a new app can't be installed to get around a lock, and with your child in your Apple Family Sharing group, CounterWait itself can't be deleted or turned off without you. No app is completely cheat-proof, and if a lock ever gets in the way, you can pause locking on their iPhone with your passcode.

What Makes CounterWait Different

  • Free to start, with no paywall after setup. Earning, camera workouts, task approvals and rules are free. Plus raises the limits to 99 people and 99 tasks each, and adds the allowance tracker, the Home Screen and Lock Screen widget, and on an adult's own iPhone, the Flip Phone Mode widget.
  • No weekly plan, and one Plus. $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year, or $59.99 once for Lifetime. Nothing hides behind a second upgrade.
  • Earning, not only blocking. Minutes come from push ups, steps and chores, so the answer to "Can I have more time?" is "Go earn it."
  • A grown-up in the loop. A parent or guardian approves a child's tasks. Adults can ask a buddy to check theirs.
  • No ads and no tracking. Your family's data stays on your phones and in your own iCloud.

We're not on social media. On purpose.

Social media is a big part of why CounterWait exists, so we don't use it. Any CounterWait page on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X or anywhere else is fake. Don't give it your information, and please report it. The only real places are counterwait.app, the App Store (Apple's TestFlight during the beta), and support@counterwait.app.

Why CounterWait Exists

The first parent to try CounterWait gave it five minutes. There was nothing to do yet, so her first try ended there. That's why setup now has you earning your first minutes right away.

She was the app developer's wife. CounterWait is made by one family in North Carolina, not a tech company. A dad wanted his kids to earn their screen time instead of only losing it, so he built a way: push ups the camera counts, chores a parent approves, steps from Apple Health. Each one turns into minutes. His wife and their kids tested it on their own phones before anyone else saw it.

The app's defaults, from minutes per push up to bedtime, are based on peer-reviewed research wherever it exists, and the app names its sources. There are no ads, no tracking and no servers. Your family's data stays on your phones and in your own iCloud. And CounterWait isn't on social media. On purpose.
