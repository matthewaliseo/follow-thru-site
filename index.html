CounterWait™

Get your wait up.

Earn your time. Lock the apps you choose and earn minutes back with exercise and tasks, on your own, as a family, or with an accountability buddy.

How it works

  1. Lock the apps. Pick the apps that eat the day, like Instagram or games. CounterWait locks them through Apple's Screen Time. For a kid, you pick them on their iPhone with your passcode.
  2. Earn minutes. Push ups, squats or jumping jacks the camera counts. Steps, workouts, Exercise minutes and mindful minutes from Apple Health. Or a chore or task a parent/guardian approves.
  3. Spend them. Tap the minutes you want. The apps open, then lock again when the time runs out.

On a kid's iPhone, the locks stay put. No app can be deleted, so a locked app can't be deleted and installed again. Changing the clock doesn't add time, and with Block New App Installs on, a new app can't be installed to get around a lock. With your kid in your Apple Family Sharing group, CounterWait itself can't be deleted or turned off without you.

What Makes CounterWait Different

  • Free to start, with no paywall after setup. Earning, camera workouts, task approvals and rules are free. Plus adds unlimited people and tasks when you want them.
  • No weekly plan, and one Plus. $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year, or once for Lifetime. Nothing hides behind a second upgrade.
  • Earning, not only blocking. Minutes come from push ups, steps and chores, so the answer to "Can I have more time?" is "Go earn it."
  • A grown-up in the loop. A parent or guardian approves a child's tasks. Adults can ask a buddy to check theirs.
  • No ads and no tracking. Your family's data stays on your phones and in your own iCloud.

Why CounterWait Exists

The first parent to try CounterWait gave it five minutes. There was nothing to do yet, so her first try ended there. That's why setup now has you earning your first minutes right away.

She was the app developer's wife. CounterWait is made by one family in North Carolina, not a tech company. A dad wanted his kids to earn their screen time instead of only losing it, so he built a way: push ups the camera counts, chores a parent approves, steps from Apple Health. Each one turns into minutes. His wife and their kids tested it on their own phones before anyone else saw it.

Every default in the app, from minutes per push up to bedtime, comes from peer-reviewed research, and the app names its sources. There are no ads, no tracking and no servers. Your family's data stays on your phones and in your own iCloud. And CounterWait isn't on social media. On purpose.
