Chore Wheel

Two wheels: names on top, chores underneath. Turn it each week and everyone moves to the next chore.

Names (top wheel)

How to put it together
  1. Write a chore in each space on the big wheel and a name in each space on the small wheel. Leave extra spaces blank, or write "Free".
  2. Cut out both wheels along the dashed lines.
  3. Lay the names wheel on top, centered, and push a paper fastener through both centers.
  4. Each week, turn the top wheel one space. Everyone moves to the next chore.
