# Be a CounterWait Buddy

Someone asked you to be their CounterWait buddy. As their buddy, you approve the tasks that earn their screen time and see when they unlock.

</div>

<div class="callout" markdown="1">

**Don't have it yet?** Get CounterWait. During setup, choose "Me, With a Buddy", then "I Help Someone", and send your invite at the end.

<p><a class="button" href="https://testflight.apple.com/join/twk486Dg">Get CounterWait</a></p>

</div>

A kid's iPhone, set up by a parent/guardian, can't be anyone's buddy. Buddies use an adult's iPhone, or a teen's own "Just Me" iPhone.

Only send your invite to someone you know. CounterWait never asks for your Apple Account password or for payment in a message.
