What's Next

Here's what we're working on after CounterWait launches. We build in the order families need it most, so this list will change as we hear from you.

Plans, not promises. Nothing here has a release date yet, and something may change or be dropped before it ships. Please choose CounterWait for what it does today. Have an idea? Email support@steppingstonegroup.net.

Up next

iPad. CounterWait on iPad, so a family's iPads can earn and lock the same way their iPhones do.

Spanish. The whole app in Spanish, checked by a native speaker.

Quicker kid setup. Fewer screens when you set up a child's iPhone with them, with the optional steps saved for later.

When a child turns 18. One button for a parent to hand the phone over. It unlocks, leaves the family, and becomes their own, with their history and reps kept.

Also planned

High Knees. A new camera exercise, counted the same way as Push Ups, Squats and Jumping Jacks.

Pet names. Name your pets in setup, so pet tasks and reminders say "Feed Biscuit" instead of "Feed the dog".

Trophy shelf. Trophies for personal bests, Face-Off wins and Phones Down, with no streaks to keep up.

A seesaw widget. A Home Screen widget that tips as minutes are earned and spent.

Invites that set the role. Someone who joins with your invite link is set up as a kid or a co-parent/guardian automatically, once you confirm it on your phone.
