The short answers about CounterWait. For setup steps and troubleshooting, see Support.

What is CounterWait?

CounterWait is an iPhone app that locks the apps you choose until you earn time for them. You earn minutes with exercise and tasks, then spend them to unlock your apps. When the time runs out, the apps lock again. It works for one adult on their own, for a family, or for two adults who keep each other accountable.

Who is CounterWait for?

Parents and guardians who want their kids to earn screen time, adults who want to cut back their own screen time, and accountability buddies, like two friends, a couple or two co-parents who check each other's tasks. It isn't a children's app: a parent or guardian, who must be 18 or older, sets it up and approves it on a child's iPhone.

How is CounterWait different from Apple's Screen Time?

CounterWait works with Apple's Screen Time and uses it to lock apps. The difference is how time is given. Apple's App Limits and Downtime set a fixed amount of time each day. In CounterWait, time is earned: walk, work out, do push ups, squats or jumping jacks in front of the camera, or finish a chore or task a parent, guardian or buddy approves. Earned minutes are spent to unlock apps for a session.

How do you earn minutes?

With steps, workouts, Exercise minutes and mindful minutes from Apple Health; with Push Ups, Squats, Jumping Jacks and Plank counted by the iPhone camera; and with chores and tasks. In a family, a parent or guardian approves a child's tasks and can ask for a photo of a finished task. A daily cap limits how much can be earned.

Does CounterWait collect my data?

Very little, and none of it for ads. CounterWait runs no servers and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It never sells or shares your information. Your minutes, tasks and settings stay on your iPhone, with a backup in your own private iCloud. What you share with family or a buddy moves between your phones through Apple's iCloud, where we can't see it. One thing does reach us: when someone opens a buddy request link or scans an approval QR code, their iPhone leaves the invite, with an iCloud user ID Apple gives your account for this app (not your name, phone number or email), in the app's public iCloud database for up to 7 days. That's why the App Store privacy label lists User ID, used only so the app works, and not for tracking. Email you send us is the only other thing we get. The full details are in the Privacy Policy at counterwait.app/privacy.html.

Does the camera record my kids?

No. When the camera counts reps, each frame is analyzed on the iPhone and then discarded. Workouts are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the camera isn't used to recognize anyone.

What does CounterWait do to protect kids?

There are no ads and no tracking. A child's iPhone never shows purchases. A parent or guardian approves CounterWait's Screen Time access on the child's iPhone with their own Apple Account, and with the child in their Apple Family Sharing group, the child can't delete CounterWait or turn off its access without them. Kids never see an adult's buddy activity. The app keeps a record of allowance owed but never holds or moves real money.

How much does CounterWait cost?

Most of CounterWait is free, including earning, camera workouts, task approvals and parent/guardian rules. The free plan includes 2 people you check, 1 co-parent/guardian and 6 tasks of your own per person. CounterWait Plus adds unlimited people and tasks, the allowance tracker and the Home Screen and Lock Screen widget. Plus is $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year, or a one-time Lifetime purchase, and it can be shared with Apple Family Sharing. The app is free during the public beta.

What do I need to use CounterWait?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, signed in to iCloud with CounterWait turned on for iCloud. Locking a child's apps needs Apple Family Sharing with the child in your family, and everyone who links up needs their own iPhone. CounterWait isn't on Android or iPad yet. iPad support is planned first.

Where can I get CounterWait?

CounterWait is in public beta on iPhone through Apple's TestFlight at testflight.apple.com/join/twk486Dg. Everyone who joins the beta must be 13 or older, which is Apple's rule for TestFlight. The App Store release is coming soon.
