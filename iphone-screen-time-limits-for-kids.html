iPhone Screen Time Limits for Kids: A Plain Guide

Updated November 21, 2026

Every iPhone comes with Screen Time, Apple's own set of limits for kids. It's free and it works. Here's what each part does, how to set it up from your own iPhone, and how to add earned time on top if you want kids to work for more.

Start with Family Sharing

Add your child to your Apple Family Sharing group, with their own Apple Account. Then you can set their limits from your iPhone, and they can't turn them off without you. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap Screen Time, and tap your child's name.

What each setting does

SettingWhat it does
Screen Time Schedule (formerly Downtime)Blocks apps during set hours, like school or bedtime. Calls and the apps you allow still work.
Time Allowances (formerly App Limits)Caps an app or a category, like Games, at a set time per day.
Always AllowedApps that never get blocked, like Phone, Messages or Maps.
Content and Privacy RestrictionsAge ratings for apps, movies and websites, and whether purchases need your okay.
Ask for More TimeYour child can ask for more time, and you approve or decline from your iPhone.

Limits that hold

  • Start with bedtime. A Screen Time Schedule overnight does the most good for the least effort.
  • Limit categories, not every app. One limit on Games is easier to keep than ten app limits.
  • Be consistent. The same rule every day works better than a strict week followed by a loose one (Pinquart, Developmental Psychology, 2017).

When kids should earn more

A fixed daily limit doesn't say anything about what comes first. If your rule is chores and homework before screens, an earned-time app can sit on top of Apple's Screen Time. CounterWait keeps the apps you pick locked until your child earns minutes with chores you approve, steps, workouts, or push-ups the iPhone camera counts. Keep the Screen Time Schedule for school hours and bedtime. Apps it blocks stay blocked even with earned minutes.

Questions

How do I set screen time limits on my child's iPhone?

Add your child to Apple Family Sharing with their own Apple Account. Then on your iPhone, open Settings, tap Screen Time, tap your child's name, and set Screen Time Schedule, Time Allowances and Content and Privacy Restrictions.

Can my child get around Screen Time?

With Family Sharing and a Screen Time passcode only you know, the limits stay on unless you change them. Keep the passcode private, and keep iOS up to date.

Can screen time limits and earned time work together?

Yes. Apple's Screen Time Schedule covers fixed hours like school and bedtime. An app like CounterWait handles earned time on top, so kids unlock their apps by doing chores or exercise first.
